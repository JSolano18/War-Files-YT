════════ DESCRIPTION A — FULL ════════
In February 2003, a vault two floors beneath Antwerp's diamond district was found open. Inside:
forced safe-deposit boxes, scattered papers, and an estimated $100 million in diamonds, gold and
jewelry gone. No explosion. No alarm. No witnesses.

This WAR FILES investigation reconstructs the Antwerp Diamond Centre heist, the record-setting
robbery behind what is often called "the heist of the century." We follow Leonardo Notarbartolo, the
Italian tenant a Belgian court later convicted of organizing it, and the so-called School of Turin.
We look at the vault's reported ten layers of security, the weekend of the break-in, and the
roadside discovery near the E19 highway that exposed the crew.

We also separate the court's findings from Notarbartolo's own disputed account: that the crew was
hired by a merchant, that the theft was an insurance fraud, and that the haul was only about
$20 million. Most of the diamonds have never been recovered.
