════════ DESCRIPTION (FULL) ════════
In April 1943, a British officer washed ashore in southern Spain with a briefcase chained to his coat. Inside were letters revealing where the Allies would invade Europe. The Germans read every word.

But Major William Martin never existed — and the man in his uniform was already dead.

This WAR FILES documentary tells the true story of Operation Mincemeat: how two British intelligence officers, Ewen Montagu and Charles Cholmondeley, turned the body of a homeless Welshman into a fictional Royal Marine, gave him a fiancée, love letters, unpaid bills and a forged mission, and sent him by submarine to the coast near Huelva. We follow the papers from a Spanish beach to German intelligence and to Hitler himself — and look honestly at what the deception did, and did not, achieve before the invasion of Sicily.

And we remember the real man behind "The Man Who Never Was": Glyndwr Michael, from Aberbargoed in South Wales.
